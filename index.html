02 / MEET YOUR PHYSIOTHERAPIST

Clinical knowledge.

Hari Varma Rajan

Founder & Lead Physiotherapist

Hari founded PT Upgraded in 2023 with a clear ambition: to make quality physiotherapy more accessible, individualised and patient-centred.

His experience spans multispecialty clinics, hospitals, paediatric rehabilitation centres, sports organisations and community-based rehabilitation. Working with people across age groups and functional abilities has shaped his approach to assessment, therapeutic exercise and rehabilitation planning.

Today, he brings together clinical knowledge, evidence-informed practice and digital healthcare to help people take an active role in their recovery. He also practises as a physiotherapist in Luxembourg.

BEFORE YOUR FIRST VISIT

A little clarity
goes a long way.

Is online physiotherapy suitable for me? +

Suitability depends on your concerns, clinical needs and location. Discuss the available consultation formats with Hari before booking. An in-person assessment may be recommended.

Can I see Hari in Luxembourg? +

Yes. Hari’s Doctena profile lists his practice at Fit and Fresh, 15 Avenue de la Faïencerie, 1510 Luxembourg. See Doctena for current appointment options and practice details.

What should I prepare for a consultation? +

Have your goals, relevant reports and any referral available. Wear comfortable clothing that allows movement. For an online session, ask the practitioner about the connection and space you will need.

Where can I check fees and availability? +

Use Hari’s Doctena profile for current booking options. Confirm fees, consultation format, referral requirements and any reimbursement arrangements directly with the practice before your appointment.
